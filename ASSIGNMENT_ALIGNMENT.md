# Assignment Alignment

The supplied assignment evaluates:
- Problem clarity and user insight — 25%
- Product thinking and MVP quality — 25%
- Evidence of traction and validation — 30%
- Speed, creativity and resourcefulness — 10%
- Learning quality and next steps — 10%

Required submission themes:
user problem, product, why AI avatar improves experience, product decisions, GTM, traction/metrics, learnings, and next tests for two more weeks.

This package is intentionally designed around those criteria.

**Important:** no traction numbers are fabricated. Validation placeholders must be replaced with real evidence.
